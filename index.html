About

I am an agricultural scientist, data specialist and certified project manager with about 20 years of experience across research, industry and engineering roles in Australia, France and Iran. My work combines crop physiology, hydrology, agrometeorology and cropping-systems modelling with data engineering, analytics and machine learning.

I have held research roles at the University of Queensland, James Cook University and the University of Southern Queensland, and earlier positions with Limagrain Europe and INRA-SupAgro in France. Before that, I worked as an engineer, senior engineer and project manager on irrigation, drainage and water-resources projects in Iran.

My research brings together crop, soil and water processes to improve productivity, resource-use efficiency and climate resilience. Combining field data, remote sensing and process-based models such as APSIM, I build tools and frameworks that support practical decisions by growers, industry and government.

Since December 2025 I have been working on independent product and data projects. Sample code is on GitHub.
